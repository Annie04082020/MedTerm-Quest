# Source-first learning for novices

Use course PDFs, transcripts and notes as course-alignment evidence. Extract source-bound atomic claims before concepts/questions. Keep directly stated facts, model interpretations and unsupported extrapolations separate. Verbatim quotation validation alone cannot prove an answer or explanation.

The learner reviews readability, usefulness and source relevance, not medical truth. Low-risk deterministic constructions may publish under Tier A. Validated Tier B exercises remain explicitly provisional, source-linked, reportable and excluded from long-term mastery. Complex or unsupported clinical conclusions remain held.

Pydantic validates local-model output on Windows. Offline Android gameplay performs deterministic answer checking and preserves unknown wording as partial/unrecognized rather than asking a novice to medically certify it. Traditional Chinese explanations and English terminology remain available with local source excerpts.

[Full tier policy and deterministic versus heuristic checks](tiered-bank-acceptance.md). Detailed original course records, quotes and learning statistics are private managed data and are not committed.
