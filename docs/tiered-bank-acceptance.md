# Source-grounded authoring policy and validation

The local Ollama pipeline extracts source-bound atomic facts across definitions, anatomy, functions, components, pathways, comparisons, mechanisms, causes and quantitative relationships. It retains source excerpts, exact page/timestamp references, terms, Traditional Chinese guidance, stable claim/concept IDs and structured evidence. Original materials are never modified.

Tier A uses deterministic source-extractive constructions. Tier B uses source-grounded local-model questions plus evidence/answer checks and is explicitly provisional. Tier C, unsafe clinical extrapolation, unsupported answers and unresolved ambiguity are quarantined. The learner never certifies medical correctness.

Deterministic checks include activity schemas, quotation/term/number/unit/formula constraints, finite mathematical constructions, duplicate prevention, literal alias/multiple-choice checks and selected known reversal regressions. Heuristics include local-model extraction, independent answer generation with the proposed answer withheld, bilingual interpretation guards, contradiction patterns and concept grouping. A second pass of the same model is not independent medical verification.

The adversarial claim `Resistance is determined by pressure difference.` must not auto-publish merely because a quotation or schema passes. Unproven Chinese interpretations may be withheld in favor of source-framed guidance and the original English fact.

Coverage counts deduplicated extracted concepts and their question variants, not paragraphs or an invented whole-lecture percentage. Limits distinguish question caps, unsupported constructions and remaining gaps. All six modes are used when suitable; superficial or ambiguous variants are held.

Generation remains sequential, cached, cancellable and resumable. No automatic transcription, model downloads or paid API calls occur. Real-course reports and imported content remain local; CI exercises public demonstration fixtures only.
